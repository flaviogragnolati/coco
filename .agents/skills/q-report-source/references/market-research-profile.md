# Market-research content profile

Load this reference only for `report.content_profile: market-research`. `report_type` still owns the communication purpose; this profile only supplies a semantic organization pattern.

Select sections from the approved scope and source coverage:

- executive synopsis;
- market definition and measurement contract;
- evidence and methodology;
- sizing, TAM/SAM/SOM, and reconciliation;
- demand, customers, and segmentation;
- market dynamics;
- competitors, shares, CRn, and HHI;
- forecast, scenarios, sensitivity, and switching values;
- regulation;
- risks, implications, and options;
- limitations and auditable appendices.

No section is mandatory when it is unrequested or unsupported. Every material block needs typed evidence refs resolving to exact versions in `source_snapshot`. Use findings for observed/source claims and Market Analysis `published_results` for calculations or scenario results. Carry qualifiers and unresolved reconciliation forward.

Treat persisted calculation JSON/CSV as derived presentation/audit data with no semantic authority. It may support a table or chart after the result is promoted, but cannot be the only evidence for a block. Reporting does not gather sources, recalculate, operate primary fieldwork, or process raw survey responses.

Complete the profile gate when every included section is requested and supported, every material block has eligible typed evidence, excluded or missing coverage is visible, and the source has no new upstream meaning.
