# Commercial document content model

## 1. Modes

### Draft

Allow explicitly classified pending information only for internal use. Keep warnings visible in the validation report. Do not release or present the draft as final.

### Issued

Require all client-facing commercial data, remove all placeholders, and require successful DOCX/PDF and visual validation.

## 2. Standard order

1. Cover.
2. Proposal metadata and contents.
3. Executive summary.
4. Background and understanding of the need.
5. Objectives.
6. Alternatives, when applicable.
7. Scope, exclusions, and assumptions.
8. Methodology and governance.
9. Work plan, deliverables, and milestones.
10. Technical or operational solution, when applicable.
11. Quality, testing, and acceptance.
12. Team.
13. Schedule.
14. Investment and payments.
15. Validity and commercial acceptance.
16. Authorized terms, when applicable.
17. Editable acceptance and signatures, when applicable.
18. Canonical change-control appendix, when applicable.

Keep the commercial part readable without a technical or legal annex. Move lengthy detail to annexes rather than densifying the executive pages.

## 3. Required relationships

- Relate the summary to the canonical problem, need, solution, duration, and investment.
- Give each scope item an objective limit.
- Keep included, excluded, and assumed content distinct.
- Give each deliverable content, format, delivery condition, and acceptance criterion.
- Link each payment to a verifiable commercial milestone or condition.
- Identify who contracts, pays, and administers each third-party service.
- Distinguish estimated dates from committed dates.
- Distinguish defects from changes and enhancements when the source defines them.
- Preserve source IDs for impact analysis without displaying every internal ID to the client.

## 4. Conditional modules

Include only when supported by the canonical source:

- Alternatives.
- Migration or initial load.
- Integrations.
- Security and personal data.
- Third-party infrastructure.
- SLA, support, and maintenance.
- Artificial intelligence use.
- Technical annexes.
- Contractual change control.
- Acceptance and signatures.

Do not leave empty headings.

## 5. Publishable content

Exclude internal margin, profitability, sales notes, negotiation strategy, private risk treatment, credentials, unnecessary personal data, and any object not referenced by the document interface or otherwise approved for publication.

## 6. Legal content

Do not use a style asset to introduce legal meaning. Require an explicit canonical reference for bundled general terms. Append only canonical particular clauses. Do not represent legal text as reviewed unless the source records the review and its authority.
