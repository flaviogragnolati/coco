# Authoring quality

- Use stable, descriptive IDs and concise labels.
- Label relationships whose meaning is not obvious.
- Keep critical rules in the caller's canonical text; diagrams may reference their IDs.
- Prefer native Mermaid syntax and avoid renderer-specific directives in `portable` and `github` profiles.
- Keep one visual question per diagram. Split when independent clusters or edge crossings obscure the main path.
- Use comments for provenance or maintenance notes, not to hide missing narrative.
- Validate the exact saved source and rerender after every repair.
