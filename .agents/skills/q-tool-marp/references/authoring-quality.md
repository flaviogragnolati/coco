# Marp authoring quality

Load this reference when mapping approved content to slides or resolving density and accessibility warnings.

- Keep one decision, conclusion, or teaching outcome per slide and use an assertion-led title when the evidence supports one.
- Prefer a short argument plus evidence over prose pages. Split a crowded slide before reducing text below the caller's legibility floor.
- Preserve units, periods, qualifiers, citations, and uncertainty exactly. Route unsupported compression or a changed conclusion to the content owner.
- Use speaker notes for delivery detail, transitions, source context, limitations, and expected decisions; do not duplicate all visible text.
- Keep a logical reading order, visible source cues, sufficient contrast, and non-color labels. Provide useful alternative text or an adjacent textual explanation for meaningful images.
- Treat line, word, and element counts as diagnostics. They can trigger review but cannot authorize deletion, summarization, or a semantic rewrite.

Complete quality review when each slide has one clear job, remains traceable to approved content, and every unresolved fit problem is returned to its owner.
