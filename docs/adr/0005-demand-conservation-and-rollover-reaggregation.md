# Demand conservation is invariant; roll overs re-enter aggregation by default

Every unit of paid demand is, at all times, in exactly one active place — unallocated original demand, an open roll over, or a live allocation — or in an audited terminal resolution (cancelled / resolved). Every quantity-moving command must preserve this inside its transaction; diagnostics watch it but never repair it. To make re-aggregation actually work (code review 2026-07-22, finding #34), the original-demand query excludes only cart items with **open** roll overs, and `Operation.includeRollOver` defaults to **true** at every layer: leaving paid demand out of an aggregation batch requires explicit opt-out, not opt-in.

Roll over statuses are: `open` (awaiting rebatch or resolution), `rebatched` (consumed by a later operation — written only by execution), `resolved` (admin action with mandatory reason for settlement outside re-aggregation; records the decision, moves no money), and `cancelled` (terminal by cascade when the underlying demand is cancelled — never a direct action). Consequence: cancelling a completed operation reverts its consumed roll overs from `rebatched` back to `open`.
